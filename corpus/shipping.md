# Shipping and Delivery

_Applies to all retail orders. Freight and pallet shipments are quoted separately._

## Service levels

| Service  | Dispatch                         | Delivery estimate   | Cost             |
|----------|----------------------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Standard | within 48 hours                  | 3-5 business days   | free over $50    |
| Express  | same day if ordered before 14:00 | 1-2 business days   | $12.00           |
| Freight  | 3-5 business days                | 10-15 business days | quoted per order |

Dispatch times are measured in business days and exclude public holidays. Orders placed on a
weekend are treated as having been placed on the following Monday for dispatch purposes.

Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 after discounts and before tax. Orders under that
threshold are charged a flat $5.95.

## Cut-off times

The express cut-off is 14:00 in the warehouse's local timezone, not the customer's. During
promotional periods the cut-off moves to 12:00 and the change is shown at checkout.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse. Tracking data can take up to 6
hours to appear on the carrier's website after the email is sent.

A parcel that shows no carrier scan 48 hours after dispatch should be reported to support, who
will open a carrier investigation.

## Address changes

An address can be changed while the order status is *preparing*. Once the status becomes
*dispatched* the address is locked and the parcel must be redirected through the carrier, which
costs $8.00.

Redirection is not available for freight shipments or for international orders that have already
cleared export.

## Delivery attempts

The carrier makes two delivery attempts on consecutive business days. After a second failed
attempt the parcel is held at a local depot for 10 days, then returned to us. A returned-to-sender
parcel is refunded less the original shipping cost once it arrives back at the warehouse.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is declared lost after 10 business days with no carrier scan. We ship a replacement at no
charge; we do not issue a refund for a lost parcel unless the item is out of stock.

Where a replacement cannot be sent because the item is out of stock, the order is refunded in full,
including the original shipping charge.

## Damaged parcels

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the packaging and the
item. A replacement ships immediately; we do not require the damaged item to be returned first for
orders under $200.

## International

We ship to 23 countries. Duties and import taxes are the recipient's responsibility and are not
included in the checkout total.

International delivery estimates assume customs clearance of 2 to 5 business days and exclude any
delay caused by incomplete recipient details, which is the most common cause of a held parcel.

## Collection

Click and collect is available at 14 partner locations. Collection orders are held for 7 days.
Photo identification matching the order name is required at collection.
