# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-14. Applies to all Acme Store orders placed on or after 2026-01-01._
_Owner: Customer Operations. Review cadence: every six months._

## Scope

This policy covers retail orders placed through the Acme Store web checkout, the mobile app, and
orders placed by phone with a support agent. It does not cover wholesale contracts, marketplace
listings fulfilled by third-party sellers, or digital subscriptions, each of which is governed by
its own agreement.

## Eligibility

A customer may request a refund within **30 days** of the delivery date. Items must be unused and
in their original packaging. Opened consumables, gift cards, and clearance items marked *final
sale* are not refundable.

Where an item was delivered in more than one parcel, the 30-day window starts from the delivery
date of the final parcel in the order.

Proof of purchase is required. The order number from the confirmation email is sufficient; a
physical receipt is not needed.

## How to start a return

1. Open the order in *My Orders* and choose **Request return**.
2. Select a reason. The reason determines who pays return shipping -- see below.
3. Print the prepaid label, or request a QR code for drop-off at a partner location.
4. Hand the parcel to the carrier within 14 days. Labels expire after that and must be reissued.

Each approved return is assigned an RMA number. Quote it in any correspondence about the refund;
support cannot trace a return without it.

## Processing time

Once a return is received at our warehouse, refunds are issued to the original payment method
within **5 business days**. Bank processing can add a further 3 to 10 days depending on the card
issuer, so the full round trip is typically under two weeks.

Refunds to store credit are issued immediately on receipt of the return.

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays in the warehouse's country of operation.
Returns received after 15:00 local time are logged the following business day.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund of up to 80% of the item price may be issued when an item is returned with
visible signs of use, or when accessories are missing from the original packaging. The warehouse
team records the condition on receipt and the deduction is itemised on the refund receipt.

A customer who disagrees with a condition assessment may request photographs of the item as
received. The warehouse retains those photographs for 90 days.

## Shipping costs

Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is a result of our error -- a wrong
item, a damaged item, or a late delivery beyond the guaranteed date. Return shipping is paid by
the customer otherwise.

Express shipping surcharges are never refunded on a change-of-mind return, even where the original
shipping charge itself is refunded.

## Exchanges

An exchange is processed as a refund plus a new order. The new order ships as soon as the return
is scanned by the carrier; the customer is not charged twice, because the replacement is created
against the original payment authorisation.

If the replacement item is out of stock, the exchange reverts to a refund automatically and the
customer is notified by email.

## Faulty items

An item that fails within the manufacturer warranty period is handled as a warranty claim, not a
return, and the 30-day window does not apply. Warranty claims are assessed by the manufacturer and
can take up to 21 days.

Where a fault is confirmed within 30 days of delivery, the customer may choose a refund, a
replacement, or a repair.

## Escalation

If a refund has not appeared 15 business days after the return was received, the customer should
contact support with the RMA number. Support can escalate to the finance team, who re-issue the
payment within 2 business days.

Escalations involving amounts over $2,000 require sign-off from the finance manager and may take
one additional business day.

## Record keeping

Refund records are retained for seven years for tax purposes. Condition photographs, carrier
scans, and agent notes attached to an RMA are retained for 90 days unless the refund is disputed,
in which case they are retained until the dispute closes.
